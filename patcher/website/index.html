Questions

Before you install

Which platforms are supported?+

Universal Patcher is offered only where The Sims 4 runs: Windows and Linux on x64 computers, and macOS on Intel and Apple silicon. No platform is offered yet.

Can it download, update or unlock the game or its packs?+

No. It works with the game and packs already on your computer, and this site does not distribute game content.

What happens to my saves and mods?+

Nothing. The patcher reads the game folder and does not write to it, and it does not open your saves or mods.

How do I update the patcher?+

The patcher does not update on its own. In the app, open Settings and choose Check for patcher update, or run this command in a terminal: universal-patcher update The new version starts the next time you open the patcher.

Is this an official EA product?+

No. Universal Patcher is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts. The Sims and related marks belong to their respective owners.

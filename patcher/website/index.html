What it does

What the app does today

Everything listed here works on an existing installation of the game.

01

Finds your game

It looks in Steam libraries on Linux, and in Steam, EA app and registry locations on Windows. If it finds nothing, you choose the folder, and it remembers your choice.

02

Lists your packs

Every pack is shown as installed, incomplete or not installed, with its size on disk and the files that are missing. Your pack and language choices are saved, but they do not change anything on disk yet.

03

Starts the game

Play starts The Sims 4 wherever it came from, a store or a plain folder. On Linux a copy in a Steam library starts through Steam, and any other copy starts through Wine. The button shows when the game is starting and when it is running.

Not included

  • Downloading or updating the game or its packs.
  • Unlocking packs.
  • Online sign-in.
  • Automatic updates of the patcher itself. See the questions below.

How to use it

Three steps on your own computer

The only thing downloaded is the patcher itself.

  1. 01

    Install

  2. 02

    Confirm your game

  3. 03

    Play

Questions

Before you install

Which platforms are supported?+

Universal Patcher is offered only where The Sims 4 runs: Windows and Linux on x64 computers, and macOS on Intel and Apple silicon. No platform is offered yet.

Can it download, update or unlock the game or its packs?+

No. It works with the game and packs already on your computer, and this site does not distribute game content.

What happens to my saves and mods?+

Nothing. The patcher reads the game folder and does not write to it, and it does not open your saves or mods.

How do I update the patcher?+

The patcher does not update on its own. In the app, open Settings and choose Check for patcher update, or run this command in a terminal: universal-patcher update The new version starts the next time you open the patcher.

Is this an official EA product?+

No. Universal Patcher is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts. The Sims and related marks belong to their respective owners.
